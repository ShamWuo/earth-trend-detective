02 · The Challenge Gap Slide 2 / 7

Seasonal Cycles Mask Long-Term Change & Hide Opposite Regional Trends

Why Naive Trend Tools Fail Students & Analysts

  • Strong Annual Seasonality: Summer-winter swings dwarf 1–2°C or 10% moisture trends, causing false slopes or missed regime shifts.
  • Opposite Regional Responses: A single coupled driver (ENSO, Arctic Amplification) pushes precipitation or temperature up in one region and down across basins.
  • Lagging & Spurious Correlations: Raw Pearson |r| mistakes synchronous winter/summer seasonality for physical causality.

Our Answer

Toggle Raw Values → Seasonal Cycle Removed on every comparison card (Hirsch-Slack Seasonal Mann-Kendall ps + climatology deseasonalization), quantify cross-basin drives_opposite arcs, and filter spurious seasonal illusions with ETG Trend Microscope.

Guided Case Studies: ENSO vs. Madagascar · Arctic vs. Subpolar Gyre · Western Fire Aridity vs. Plains River · Front Range vs. Divide Deseasonalized Non-Parametric Tests
